Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE — to be set at launch]

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Jelly Gates (the "Game"), published by Wakeup Monster ("we", "us"). Our legal entity name and postal address: [LEGAL NAME, ADDRESS — to be provided].

Note for the owner: this page was written without access to the in-app privacy text and must be reconciled with it before launch.

1. Short version

  • There is no account, no login and no cloud save. You play as a guest.
  • Your progress, settings and coins are stored on your device only.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Our server checks purchases using a purchase token and product id, nothing personal.

2. Data stored on your device only

Game progress, levels completed, coin balance, purchased items, settings and similar data live in local storage on your device. We do not receive a copy. Uninstalling the Game removes this data (see Delete your data).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from IP), and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalise ads. Learn more in Google's partner policy.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings (privacy options), where offered.
  • iOS: we ask permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, the Game does not access your IDFA for tracking and ads may be less relevant.
  • You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing and Apple App Store)

Purchases (coin packs, extra moves, remove ads, cosmetics) are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit. Payment details are handled solely by Google or Apple; we never see or store your card or payment information. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

5. Purchase verification (Firebase Cloud Functions)

To confirm that a purchase is genuine and to deliver it, the Game sends the store purchase token and product id to our backend running on Google Firebase Cloud Functions. This request contains no name, email or other personal details. We keep these verification records for as long as needed to prevent fraud, handle refunds and meet legal obligations. Firebase, as our infrastructure provider, may process technical data such as IP address in server logs.

6. Analytics

We do not include a separate analytics SDK. Only the data collected by AdMob and Firebase as described above is processed.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help. [Owner decision to confirm: age statement.]

8. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. California residents may also request to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; we do not sell personal information for money, and ad personalisation is controlled by the consent and device settings above. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests are answered by explaining that data is on your device. To exercise rights, e-mail support@example.com. We will respond within the period required by law (generally 30 days).

9. International transfers and security

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside your own under their own safeguards. We use reasonable measures to protect our backend, but no system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and, for material changes, we will notify you in the Game or on this page.
