Support

Need a hand with Jelly Gates? Start with the answers below, or e-mail us at support@example.com.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game, go to Settings → Restore purchases (path to be confirmed against the app). Use the same Google Play or Apple ID you bought with. Purchases such as "remove ads" are tied to your store account, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new device. Consumable items (e.g. coin packs) already spent can't be restored.

I lost my progress. Can you get it back?

Jelly Gates has no login and no cloud save, so progress lives only on your device. Uninstalling the game, clearing app data, or changing phones removes it, and we can't recover it because we never receive a copy. Your purchases can be restored (see above). Check whether your phone's own device backup is enabled to carry app data to a new device.

Why are there ads? Can I remove them?

Ads (via Google AdMob) help keep the game going. Rewarded ads are optional and give you bonuses. You can buy Remove ads in the shop to remove interstitial ads. See the Privacy Policy for how ads use data and how to change your ad choices.

My purchase didn't arrive.

Check your connection and reopen the game; purchases are verified on our server and delivered when the check completes. If it still hasn't arrived, e-mail us with your store order number (never your card details) and the item name.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple. Request them through your store account's purchase history.
